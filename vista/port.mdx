A **Port** represents your physical cross-connect entry point into the Polarin high-performance optical network (available in 1 Gbps, 10 Gbps, and 100 Gbps port speeds). Because the port forms the physical anchor upon which all Cloud Connect connections, Virtual Routers, and Data Centre Interconnects run, monitoring its health is vital.

VISTA for Port delivers continuous, real-time telemetry on transceiver optical levels, interface frame rates, physical link state transitions, and bandwidth saturation.

## Optical Power & Hardware Health

Physical optical interfaces depend on proper laser transmission levels. VISTA reads digital optical monitoring (DOM) counters directly from the data Centre switch transceiver:

- **Tx Power (Transmit)**: The optical signal power generated by the Polarin switch laser towards your patch panel.
- **Rx Power (Receive)**: The optical power received from your cross-connect. If Rx power drops below transceiver thresholds, VISTA alerts you to optical attenuation, bent patch cords, or dust contamination.
- **Link Stability (Flaps)**: Monitors physical link state transitions. A reliable port maintains 0 flaps over a 24-hour period.

## Port Metrics Reference

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Traffic In (Mbps) | Ingress throughput entering the Polarin port from the customer premises cross-connect. |
| Traffic Out (Mbps) | Egress throughput exiting the Polarin port towards customer equipment. |
| Packets In & Out | Total count of Ethernet frames forwarded through the physical interface. |
| Optical Power (dBm) | Transmit (Tx) and Receive (Rx) optical decibel levels for transceiver diagnostics. |
| Flaps Counter | Number of physical link transitions between Up and Down states in the last 24 hours. |
| Error Drops / CRC | Hardware packet drop counters caused by CRC errors or physical layer signal distortion. |

## Troubleshooting Port Metrics

- **Zero Traffic on a Live Port**: If your port is _Live_ but displays zero throughput, check that your local switch interface is un-shutdown and transmitting tagged VLAN packets.
- **Non-Zero Flap Counts**: If flap counts increment, inspect physical fiber patches at the colocation facility or raise a ticket via the **Raise an Issue** button on the port header.
