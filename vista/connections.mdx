Cloud Connect and DCI Layer 2 connections both deliver dedicated, private point-to-point bandwidth across the Polarin software-defined network. Whether connecting a data Centre to public cloud on-ramps, bridging multi-cloud environments, or linking two of your own sites over Ethernet, VISTA provides end-to-end telemetry on connection performance, packet integrity, and latency.

VISTA for Connections combines continuous performance monitoring with self-service bandwidth scaling through the **Effective Rate Limit** engine.

## Supported Connection Types

VISTA telemetry applies identically across all of Polarin's point-to-point connection products:

- **DC to Cloud (Cloud Connect)**: Private, direct interconnection to hyperscalers (AWS Direct Connect, Microsoft Azure ExpressRoute, Google Cloud Interconnect, Oracle FastConnect).
- **Cloud to Cloud**: Low-latency interconnects linking workloads running across different cloud service providers.
- **DCI Layer 2**: Dedicated Ethernet LAN extension across data Centres for stretched clusters and storage replication.

## Effective Rate Limit & Dynamic Bandwidth Scaling

VISTA pairs deep telemetry with instantaneous bandwidth scaling. On any active connection, the **EFFECTIVE RATE LIMIT** card displays your active bandwidth ceiling:

![VISTA Effective Rate Limit and Health KPIs on a point-to-point connection](/images/screenshots/vista/03-vista-telemetry-rate-limit.jpg)

VISTA: (1) Effective Rate Limit with Base + Temp Add-on, (2) Health KPIs (Flaps, Latency, Availability 100%), (3) End-to-End A-End to Z-End topology.

- **Base Rate Limit (Badge 1)**: Your contracted monthly baseline subscription (e.g., `1 Mbps @ ₹3,617.50/mo`). Click **Upgrade** to adjust your permanent monthly bandwidth tier.
- **Temp Add-on (Badge 1)**: On-demand burst capacity added dynamically (e.g., `+22 Mbps`) for seasonal traffic spikes, data migrations, or unexpected workloads. Click **Edit** to increase or decrease temporary bandwidth in seconds.
- **Total Effective Bandwidth**: The combined throughput enforced across the link (e.g., `23 Mbps`). Rate limit modifications take effect immediately in the data plane without dropping packets or renegotiating BGP sessions.

## Real-Time Telemetry & Health

Beneath the rate limit card, VISTA continuously audits your circuit's SLA health over the last 24 hours:

- **Flaps**: Tracks interface state transitions. A reliable connection maintains `0 flaps`.
- **Latency RTD (Round Trip Delay)**: Millisecond-level round-trip propagation transit time across endpoints.
- **Availability (%)**: Uptime percentage evaluated against Polarin's 99.99% SLA commitment (e.g. `100.00%`).

### End-to-End A-End to Z-End Topology

The topology section maps the physical data Centre endpoints anchoring your connection:

- **A-END - PORT**: The originating port and facility (e.g. _NTT Bengaluru DC3, Bidarahalli Hobli_).
- **Z-END - PORT**: The destination port and facility (e.g. _STT GDC Chennai DC1, VSB Sivananda Salai_).
- Click **View Details** to inspect physical patch IDs, optical transceivers, and cross-connect patch cords.

## Metrics Reference

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Traffic In & Out (Mbps) | Bidirectional throughput transmitted across the virtual connection in megabits per second. |
| Packet In & Out | Count of Ethernet frames forwarded across the virtual circuit. |
| Latency RTD (ms) | Round Trip Delay measured continuously between A-End and Z-End endpoints. |
| Jitter (ms) | Statistical variance in packet arrival delay (Premium Package). Critical for voice and real-time streaming. |
| Packet Loss (%) | Percentage of transmitted frames that failed to reach the destination interface (Premium Package). |
| Errors Report | Detailed breakdown of CRC errors, frame alignment errors, and interface drops (Premium Package). |
| Availability (180 Days) | Historical uptime percentage across 6 months for SLA compliance verification (Premium Package). |
| Effective Rate Limit | Active throughput ceiling (Base contracted rate limit + Temporary Add-on bandwidth). |

## Troubleshooting & Support

- **Traffic Drops at Rate Limit**: If traffic graphs plateau at your configured rate limit, your application is saturating capacity. Click **Edit** under Temp Add-on to instantly add bandwidth.
- **Sudden Latency Spikes**: Check the _Latency RTD_ graph. If latency suddenly doubles, an optical protection switch or reroute may have occurred. Check the _Tickets_ panel for carrier maintenance notices.
