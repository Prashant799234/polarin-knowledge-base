Once your organisation clears verification, its profile lives here — a permanent, editable record rather than the one-time setup wizard you filled out in [Complete Your Profile](/get-started/organisation/complete-profile). A **Verified** badge confirms your account is fully approved and can deploy services globally. **①** below is the sidebar of four sub-sections; **②** is the content for whichever one is selected.

![Organisation Profile page with Organization Details tab selected](/images/screenshots/org-settings/01-org-details.jpg)

## Organization Details

| Field | Description | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Country Registered In | Where your organisation is legally registered. | Yes |
| Company Name | Your organisation's registered legal name. | Yes |
| Address | State/Province, City, and Postal Code. | Yes |
| Legal Entity Type | Individual, Partnership, LLP, Private/Public Limited, or Trust/Society. | Yes |
| Proof of Identity | The KYC document for your country and entity type — see requirements below. | Yes |

Click **Edit** to update these. You have two ways to fill in your address:

- **GST Verification (Recommended)** — enter your GSTIN and Polarin instantly auto-fills your Company Name, Address, State/Province, City, and Postal Code from it.
- **Manual Details** — type everything in yourself, if you'd rather not use GST lookup or don't have one.

<Note>
  Updating any of this sends your profile **back for re-approval** — you won't be able to place new orders until it's re-approved. Existing services and orders are unaffected in the meantime.
</Note>

Not sure which document your country and entity type need? See [KYC Document Requirements](/get-started/organisation/kyc-documents).

## PO Settings

A simple Yes/No: does your organisation issue a **Purchase Order** for invoicing? If yes, every invoice will expect a matching PO — see how that plays out during ordering in [Understanding the Service Detail Page](/service-management/service-detail).

<Tip>
  Changing this setting only applies to **new** purchases — anything already ordered keeps running under whatever PO setting was active when it was placed.
</Tip>

## Authorised Signatory Details

The person empowered to sign contracts on your organisation's behalf. This is the same signatory collected during initial setup — update it here any time their details change. **①** below is the read-only PO Settings summary, and **②** the Authorised Signatory card beneath it.

![PO Settings and Authorised Signatory Details sections](/images/screenshots/org-settings/02-po-signatory.jpg)

Clicking **Edit** opens the update form: **①** is the contact fields — Name, Email ID, Phone Number, and optional Department — and **②** is where you attach two supporting documents.

![Update Authorised Signatory Details drawer with contact fields and document uploads](/images/screenshots/org-settings/03-signatory-drawer.jpg)

| Field | Description | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Full name of the person authorised to sign contracts for your organisation. | Yes |
| Email ID | Their contact email. | Yes |
| Phone Number | With country code. | No |
| Designation | Their role or title at the organisation. | No |
| Department | Optional. | No |

The two supporting documents:

- **Proof of Identity of Authorised Signatory**
- **Board Resolution (Power of Attorney)** confirming their authority to sign

Both accept PDF, JPG, or PNG, up to 5.0 MB each.

## Terms & Conditions

Shows whether you've accepted Polarin's terms, how (typically **Accepted Online**), and exactly when — down to the minute. The accepted documents themselves, the Polarin Terms & Conditions and the Polarin Service Schedule, are both available to download from here at any time.

![Terms and Conditions section showing accepted status and documents](/images/screenshots/org-settings/04-terms.jpg)
