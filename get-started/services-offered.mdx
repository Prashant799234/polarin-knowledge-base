## Port

A **Port** is your organisation's physical entry point into the Polarin network, available at 1GE, 10GE, or 100GE. It's the first thing you order at a new location - everything else (virtual connections, routers, cloud links) attaches to a port. See [Create a Port](/products/port/create) for the full walkthrough.

- Choose a speed that matches your current and near-term bandwidth needs.
- Group multiple ports into a [Link Aggregation Group](/products/port/lag) for higher throughput and failover.

## Virtual Router

A **Virtual Router** is your L3 gateway for routing traffic between clouds, data centres, and partner networks, without deploying and maintaining physical routing hardware yourself. See [Create a Virtual Router](/products/virtual-router/create) to get started.

- Useful once you're connecting more than two endpoints and need real routing logic between them.
- Sits on top of a port - provision the port first, then attach a virtual router.

## Cloud Connect

A **Cloud Connect** gives you a private, point-to-point link to a cloud provider - DC to Cloud or Cloud to Cloud - bypassing the public internet for lower latency and more predictable performance than a standard VPN. See [What Is Cloud Connect?](/products/cloud-connect/overview) for the full breakdown.

- Two types: DC to Cloud (a port you own reaching a cloud provider) and Cloud to Cloud (two cloud providers linked via a Virtual Router).
- Better suited to steady, high-bandwidth workloads than internet-based connectivity.

## Data Centre Interconnect (DCI)

DCI links two or more of your data centre sites together at high bandwidth - for replication, disaster recovery, or simply treating multiple sites as one extended network. See [Create a Data Centre Interconnect](/products/dci/compare) to get started.

- **DCI Wave**: dedicated, point-to-point optical connectivity (Layer 1) between two entire sites - the highest, most predictable throughput, with no port required.
- **DCI Layer 2**: an Ethernet-based connection over two of your own ports - the more common choice, since it rides on infrastructure you've likely already provisioned.

## Internet Exchange

**Internet Exchange** connects your Port to Polarin's peering fabric, so you exchange traffic directly with other networks over BGP instead of routing it through an upstream transit provider. See [What Is Internet Exchange?](/products/internet-exchange/overview) for the full breakdown.

- **Multilateral (route-server) peering**: one BGP session picks up every other participant on the exchange at once.
- **Bilateral peering**: a direct, one-to-one BGP session with a specific network.

<Tip>
  Not sure which service you need first? Most organisations start with a **Port**, then add a **Virtual Router** or **Cloud Connect** once they know what they're connecting to. [Quick Setup](/get-started/quick-setup) walks through the order.
</Tip>

## Managing What You Order

Once a service is ordered, its progress - from design through to live - shows up on the Services page, so you always know what's ready to use and what's still provisioning. All of it also feeds into the **SPOG dashboard** for a single view of usage and performance across every service you run.
