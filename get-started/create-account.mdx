Polarin is a software-defined networking platform that enables enterprises and carriers to provision virtual connections to cloud providers, data centres, and partners - all from a single portal. Creating your account is the first step; signing up only creates your _personal login_ - your organisation itself still needs to be verified afterward, which is covered in [Complete Organisation Profile](/get-started/organisation/complete-profile).

<Note>
  Signing up does not log you in automatically. You must verify your email first, then sign in separately - both steps are covered below.
</Note>

## Create Your Account

Open the Polarin sign-up page - branded **"India's First True NaaS Platform"** - and fill in the form:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Name \* | Your full name as it should appear on the account. |
| Email Address \* | A valid work email address. This will be used for account verification and sign-in. |
| Organization \* | Your company or organisation name. As you type, the form checks it against organisations already on Polarin. |
| Password \* | Must satisfy every rule listed live under the field - see Password Policy below. |
| Confirm Password \* | Must match the Password field exactly. |

![Polarin sign up form with live password requirement checklist](/images/screenshots/signup/01-signup-form.jpg)

Click **Sign Up** once every field (and every password rule) is satisfied.

<Warning>
  If the organisation name you enter already exists on Polarin, the form will flag it - use a different, more specific name, or ask whoever manages that organisation to [invite you](/settings/user-management) instead of signing up separately.
</Warning>

## Verify Your Email

Submitting the form takes you straight to an **"Email sent successfully!"** confirmation screen - it names the inbox it was sent to and offers a **Resend link** countdown if it doesn't arrive.

![Email sent successfully confirmation screen with resend countdown](/images/screenshots/signup/02-email-sent.jpg)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Look for an email titled **"Thank you for signing up to Polarin"**. Check spam/junk if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Verify My Email">
    The button is unique to your account and expires **24 hours** after signup - a plain-text link is also included underneath in case the button doesn't render.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on the Email Verified confirmation">
    You'll see a short "Welcome to Polarin" / "See What's Possible!" confirmation - from here you [sign in](/get-started/sign-in) separately, you are not logged in automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Password Policy

The sign-up form checks your password live, item by item, as you type. It must include:

- Minimum **8 characters**
- At least one **uppercase** letter (A-Z)
- At least one **number** (0-9)
- At least one **special character** (e.g. @, #, \$, !)

<Tip>
  Use a passphrase - a string of 3-4 random words mixed with a number and symbol - for a strong yet memorable password (e.g. _Sky9Mango!River_).
</Tip>
