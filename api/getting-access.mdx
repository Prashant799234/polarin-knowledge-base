The Polarin API is available to all active Polarin customers. Here is the full journey, from sign-up to your first API call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register on Polarin">
    Sign up at the Polarin portal with your company name, primary contact, and required services. Takes about 5 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete KYC">
    Upload business registration, director ID, and proof of address. Reviewed within 1–2 business days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Account Activated">
    Once KYC is approved, your account goes live with all ordered services accessible in the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Activation Email">
    You will get your portal login credentials and initial staging API key directly to your registered email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start with Staging">
    Test with your staging credentials, no real services, no billing. Contact your account manager for production access.

    <Tip>
      First API call in under 30 minutes from activation.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

For the full API reference, visit the [Developer Portal](/developer).
