The **Password** card on your [Profile](/account/personal-information) page displays your masked password, verifies that it satisfies Polarin's security standard (_Your current password meets all security requirements_), and indicates the date on which it was last changed. Click **Update Password** to rotate your credentials at any time.

## Password Requirements

Before Polarin accepts a new password, it must satisfy all four complexity requirements:

- Password should have 8 characters.
- At least 1 upper case letter
- At least 1 number
- At least 1 special character

<Info>
  The update form checks each requirement dynamically as you type your new password. As each condition is satisfied, its corresponding indicator ticks off in real time, making it easy to identify any missing rule before submitting.
</Info>

## Update Your Password

Clicking **Update Password** opens the secure password update drawer from the right of the screen:

![Update Your Password drawer showing old password, new password with checklist, confirm field, and update button](/images/screenshots/profile/03-update-password.jpg)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Update Password">
    On the Profile page, click **Update Password** to open the drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Old Password">
    Type your current password into the **Old Password** field **(1)**. Use the eye icon toggle on the right if you need to reveal and verify what you typed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your New Password">
    Type your new password into the **New Password** field **(2)**. Watch the live checklist beneath the field confirm each rule as you meet it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your New Password">
    Re-enter the exact same password into the **Confirm New Password** field **(3)**. Both passwords must match before the form can be submitted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Update Password">
    Once your old password is provided, all 4 complexity rules are met, and both new password entries match, the **Update Password** button **(4)** turns active. Click it to finalize your update.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  We recommend rotating your account password periodically, and immediately if you ever suspect your device, email, or credentials have been compromised.
</Tip>

## Password Expiry

Polarin supports scheduled password expiry to help enterprise customers meet their own compliance requirements. If your organisation has this enabled, you'll see proactive reminder emails in the lead-up to your expiry date -- it's designed to not come as a surprise.

<Tip>
  If your password does expire before you get to it, you're not locked out without a path back: a self-service password reset link is issued directly to your registered email, so you can regain access without raising a support ticket.
</Tip>
