The **Profile** page under **Settings > Profile** gives you direct visibility and control over your individual login identity, security credentials, and account protection. Unlike [Organisation Details](/get-started/organisation/settings) (which applies to your entire company and billing tenancy), settings here are strictly personal to your user account.

![Polarin Profile page overview showing personal information, password, and two-factor authentication cards](/images/screenshots/profile/01-profile-overview.jpg)

## Profile Sections

The main Profile screen organizes your account preferences into three dedicated cards:

- **(1) Personal Information**: Displays your contact details, avatar, and system role. Click **Edit** to modify your name or phone number.
- **(2) Password**: Displays your masked password, security compliance indicator (_Your current password meets all security requirements_), and the date it was last rotated. Click **Update Password** to change it.
- **(3) Two-factor authentication**: Displays whether multi-factor security is currently active. Polarin recommends Google Authenticator or Duo for instant code verification. Click **Set up Two-Factor Authentication** to configure app- or email-based 2FA.

## Update Personal Information

Clicking the **Edit** button on the Personal Information card opens the slide-out drawer where you can update your profile name and contact number:

![Update Your Personal Information slide-out drawer](/images/screenshots/profile/02-update-personal-info.jpg)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Edit on the Personal Information card">
    The **Update Your Personal Information** drawer slides open from the right side of the screen with your current details pre-populated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your Name">
    Type your preferred display name in the **Name** field **(1)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide or update your Phone Number">
    Choose your international calling country code from the dropdown menu, then enter your mobile number in the **Phone Number** field **(3)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review locked fields (Email ID & Role)">
    **Email ID (2)** and **Role (4)** are intentionally non-editable in this drawer. Because email addresses are bound to active authentication tokens and roles determine administrative privileges across Polarin, changes must be processed through an administrator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Update to save">
    Click the **Update** button **(5)** at the bottom right. Your profile updates immediately without requiring approval.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Field Reference

| Field | Description | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Your display name across the Polarin console. Fully editable. | Yes |
| Email ID | The primary email address tied to your login. Locked and read-only in this form -- email changes must be requested through Support or your organisation's administrator. | No |
| Phone Number | Select your country dial code from the dropdown, then enter your mobile number. Used for account recovery and emergency notifications. | No |
| Role | Read-only. Reflects your assigned permission level (e.g. Network Admin) set under User Management by your organisation's administrator. | No |

<Info>
  Need to change the email address tied to your login? Reach out to your organisation's Polarin administrator or contact Polarin Support. For role upgrades (such as gaining Service Ordering or Billing access), see [User Management](/settings/user-management).
</Info>
