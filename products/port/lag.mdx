A **Link Aggregation Group (LAG)** bundles multiple physical ports into a single logical connection, giving you higher aggregate bandwidth and built-in redundancy — if one member port fails, traffic redistributes across the rest. On Polarin, LAG isn't a separate product with its own wizard: it's a toggle inside the [Create a Port](/products/port/create) flow itself.

## What is a LAG?

LAG (also known as port channel, or IEEE 802.3ad link aggregation) combines up to 8 physical ports into one logical interface. The result is:

- **Higher bandwidth** — aggregate speeds across multiple ports (e.g. ×4 on a 10 Gbps port gives a 40 Gbps LAG).
- **Built-in redundancy** — traffic fails over to surviving ports if one goes down.

## Before You Begin

- Your **Organisation Profile** must be verified.
- Your edge device must support **IEEE 802.3ad LACP** to actually use the bundled link.
- Decide how many member ports you want up front — all of them share the same bandwidth tier and the same physical location.

<Note>
  The LAG toggle is only available while configuring a port, not after it's already been ordered. Decide on your LAG size before you reach the Configure Port step.
</Note>

## Enable LAG on a Port

Start a port order exactly as described in [Create a Port](/products/port/create): choose a location, then on the **Configure Port** step, pick the port's name and bandwidth as usual. Below that sits the **LAG (Link Aggregation Group)** toggle — switch it on and a row of ×1–×8 tiles appears.

| Field | Description | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LAG (Link Aggregation Group)** | A toggle inside the Configure Port step. Off by default — switch it on to turn this port order into a LAG. | Yes |
| **Member Count & Bandwidth** | Once LAG is on, pick from ×1 through ×8 tiles. Each tile is both the number of physical ports bundled and the resulting aggregate bandwidth at the per-port speed you picked (e.g. ×4 on a 1 Gbps port = a 4 Gbps LAG across 4 ports). | Yes |
| **Port Bandwidth** | Set once, before the LAG toggle — all member ports in the LAG share this same speed (1, 10, or 100 Gbps). | Yes |

Everything after that — Add Ons (Cross Connect, VISTA), Subscription Term, Payment Options, and Checkout — works exactly the same as a standalone port order. The only difference is the price, which scales with the number of member ports.
