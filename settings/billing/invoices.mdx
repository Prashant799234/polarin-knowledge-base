**Invoices** lives in the top navigation bar alongside Dashboard, Services, Settings, and Help -- not under Settings like most other billing screens. It lists every invoice generated for your organisation, with search, date filtering, and one-click PDF downloads.

## Before Your First Invoice

If nothing has been billed yet, the page shows an empty state instead of a table:

![Invoices page empty state showing 'No Invoice Generated Yet!'](/images/screenshots/billing/01-invoices-empty.jpg)

Invoices are generated once your [Billing Cycle](/settings/billing/overview) runs its first cycle against a **Live** service -- see [Invoice Lifecycle](/settings/billing/overview) for how that timing works.

## The Invoices List

Once invoices exist, the page lists them in a table:

![Invoices list with Invoice No, Invoice Date, Invoice Total Amount, PO Number, Billing Profile, and Actions columns](/images/screenshots/billing/02-invoices-list.jpg)

- **Invoice No** and **Invoice Date** identify and timestamp the invoice -- dates currently display as a raw timestamp rather than a formatted date.
- **Invoice Total Amount** and **PO Number** are both sortable -- click the arrows in either column header to reorder the list.
- **Billing Profile** can be filtered via the funnel icon in its column header, useful if your organisation bills against more than one [Billing Profile](/settings/billing/profile).

## Search & Date Filter

- **Search by Invoice ID** -- type or paste an invoice number to jump straight to it.
- **Start Date / End Date** -- click either field to open a dual-month calendar. Pick a start day, then an end day; the range between them highlights automatically. The swap icon between the two fields flips start and end.

![Date range calendar picker open over the invoices list, showing a selected range from October 1 to November 29](/images/screenshots/billing/03-invoices-date-filter.jpg)

<Info>
  **Apply** only lights up once a full range is selected -- picking just a start date isn't enough to filter.
</Info>

## Downloading an Invoice

Click the download icon in the **Actions** column to save that invoice as a PDF. The file downloads with a long, system-generated filename rather than the invoice number.

<Warning>
  If your browser reports something like _"Insecure download blocked"_ instead of saving the file, try again or use a different browser -- this is a browser-side download-security check, not a sign that anything is wrong with your invoice. If it keeps happening, raise it via [Create a Ticket](/support/create-ticket).
</Warning>

## Field Reference

| Field | Description | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invoice No | The unique invoice number, formatted like a short reference code (e.g. 26-27LTCKAP01004). | No |
| Invoice Date | When the invoice was generated, shown as a full timestamp with timezone offset. | No |
| Invoice Total Amount | The total billed amount in your billing currency. Sortable -- click the column header to toggle ascending/descending. | No |
| PO Number | The purchase order reference for that invoice, if your organisation issues one. Sortable. | No |
| Billing Profile | The billing profile the invoice was issued against, shown by its internal ID. Filterable via the funnel icon in the column header. | No |
| Actions | A download icon that saves the invoice as a PDF. | No |
