Every action across your Polarin organisation leaves a trace -- sign-ins, service changes, billing updates, configuration edits. The **Activity Log** turns that into a single, searchable record, so you never have to wonder what happened or who did it.

![Polarin Activity Log table overview](/images/screenshots/activity-logs/01-activity-logs-table.jpg)

## Why This Exists

We built this so you can track everything that happens on your account, without asking anyone.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Accountability" icon="users">
    See exactly who on your team did what, and when -- every login, every change.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Faster Troubleshooting" icon="search">
    When something changes unexpectedly, the log shows the exact event and timing behind it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Security Visibility" icon="shield-check">
    Spot unusual sign-ins or actions early -- including the IP, location, and device behind them.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Nothing Gets Lost" icon="eye">
    Actions taken by Polarin's support team on your behalf show up here too -- full transparency.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  The bigger your team, the more this matters -- the Activity Log is what lets multiple people safely share one organisation without losing track of who changed what.
</Tip>

## How It Works

- Every event is listed with its **service**, **timestamp**, who **initiated** it, and a **severity** level from S0 (Emergency) to S7 (Debugging).
- Filter by service, date range, or severity, or search directly by Activity ID, to narrow thousands of events down to the one you need.
- Click any entry to see its full detail -- including the IP address, location, browser, and device behind it.

See [Using Activity Log](/settings/activity-logs/using-activity-log) for the full walkthrough -- where to find it, how to filter, and what every severity level means.
