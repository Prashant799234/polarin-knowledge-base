Every service you provision through Polarin (a Port, a Cloud Connect connection, a Virtual Router, a DCI connection) opens into the same kind of detail page. Once you know how to read one, you know how to read all of them.

Open it from the **Services** list on the left, or by selecting the service from any list showing your services. The header always shows the service's name, its current status badge (see [Understanding Service Status](/service-management/service-status)), when it was created, and its unique Service ID. Click the copy icon next to the ID to copy it. This is useful when raising a [ticket](/support/create-ticket) about a specific service.

## The Tabs

Not every service shows every tab. Which tabs appear depends on the product and its current status.

| Tab | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Track Order | Only shown while a service is still being provisioned; a step-by-step timeline from order validation through to going live. |
| Overview | The service's rate limit, any add-ons, its A-End and Z-End connection points, and subscription/PO details once billing starts. |
| Connections | Port only — every service currently running over that Port, plus a shortcut to add another. |
| Subscription | Plan, term, renewal, and subscription-specific details for this service. |
| Invoices & Payments | This month's estimated charge, when the next invoice generates, and a full cost breakdown. |

**1** below is the header row you'll see on almost every service: status, created date, **Raise a Ticket**, and **Edit** (or, for a Port, **Add Connection** instead). **2** is the tab bar itself, right underneath.

## Track Order

While a service is still being provisioned, its detail page opens straight to a timeline like this one:

![Track Order tab showing the provisioning timeline for a Port](/images/screenshots/services/05-track-order.jpg)

**1** The tab bar with Track Order active — **2** Purchase Order Required step — **3** Cross Connect in process.

- **Order Details Validated** — your order information has been reviewed and approved.
- **Purchase Order Required** — appears only if your Organisation issues POs for invoicing (see [Organisation Settings](/get-started/organisation/settings)). Provide it before the stated deadline via **Update PO Details**, or provisioning stalls here.
- **Deployed** — shown as "Deployment in Progress" on the status badge while it's underway, and as "Port deployed" (or the equivalent for your product) once it completes. Either way, this is the step where Polarin actually provisions the resource.
- **Ready to Patch** — for a Port specifically, Lightstorm is arranging the physical cross-connect at the data Centre, shown as **Cross Connect** with an "In process" badge.
- **Live** — the final step. Once reached, the Track Order tab disappears and Overview becomes the default.

<Tip>
  Each completed step shows the exact date and time it happened. This is useful context to have ready if you raise a ticket about a delay.
</Tip>

## The Overview Tab

**1** is the **Rate Limit** banner — your provisioned bandwidth. If a temporary bandwidth add-on is active, it shows alongside as its own line (as **Temp Add-on** below), editable or removable independently of the base rate.

![Effective Rate Limit banner with a temporary bandwidth add-on](/images/screenshots/services/03-rate-limit-addon.jpg)

**1** Tab bar with Track Order still present — **2** Effective Rate Limit, split into base rate and a Temp Add-on.

Below that, the **A-End** and **Z-End** cards show the two points a service connects. For a Port, that's just one end; for a Cloud Connect connection or DCI, both ends are shown side by side.

![Service Overview tab with rate limit, A-End/Z-End, and subscription details](/images/screenshots/services/02-overview-detail.jpg)

**1** Rate Limit — **2** A-End / Z-End connection points — **3** Subscription Details.

<Info>
  Until a service actually goes live, you'll see a note that **subscription billing hasn't started yet**. Dates and charges only apply once the connection is live, not from when you placed the order.
</Info>

Further down, **PO Details** shows your Purchase Order number and dates if your Organisation uses them. These are editable inline via their own **Update** link.

## Subscription & Invoices

The **Invoices & Payments** tab breaks down exactly what this service will cost:

![Invoices and Payments tab with cost breakdown](/images/screenshots/services/04-invoices.jpg)

**1** Estimated total for this month — **2** next invoice date — **3** line-by-line breakdown (base + VISTA).

- **Estimated This Month** — base rate plus any add-ons, combined into one number.
- **Invoice generation date** — exactly when the next invoice will be raised.
- **Current Invoice Amount Breakdown** — the base connection charge, plus any VISTA monitoring tier attached to it, itemised separately.
- **Estimated Monthly, One-time Upfront, and Add-ons Monthly** — shown as three separate totals underneath, so nothing is bundled together unexpectedly.

## Finding a Service

Every service list has a search box plus quick filter chips for **Live** and **Down**. For anything more specific, **Advance Filter** opens a full panel:

![Advance Filter panel with status and term options](/images/screenshots/services/06-advance-filter.jpg)

**1** Filter by any status — **2** filter by subscription term, from PAYG to 60 months.

Beyond Status and Term, it also filters by **Subscription** (Active/Terminated) and a **Created Date Range** — useful for finding, say, everything ordered last quarter that's still sitting in Design.

## Actions on This Page

- **Edit** — change the service's configuration where still permitted for its current status.
- **Raise a Ticket** — opens a ticket pre-linked to this specific service, so support has the right context immediately. See [Create a Ticket](/support/create-ticket).
- **Add Connection** — Port only, from the Connections tab, to attach another service to that Port without leaving the page.
- The **⋮** menu next to Edit holds less common actions specific to that service type.
