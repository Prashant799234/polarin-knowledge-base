The bell icon in the top navigation opens the **Alerts & Notifications** slide-over drawer — your central activity hub tracking both real-time network telemetry alarms and account lifecycle milestones. A red badge on the bell highlights whenever new unread items require your attention.

## Alerts vs Notifications

The drawer separates platform events into two dedicated tabs so operational alarms never get buried under routine account activity:

- **Alerts** — telemetry-driven warnings and threshold breaches generated by VISTA, such as sudden availability drops, bandwidth saturation, or latency spikes.
- **Notifications** — service provisioning updates, order confirmations, subscription renewals, and lifecycle transitions across all your connections and ports.

![Alerts & Notifications drawer showing the Notifications tab](/images/screenshots/notifications/01-notifications-tab.jpg)

## Order & Provisioning Updates

Selecting the **Notifications** tab **①** displays a chronological log of your services moving through each stage of deployment. Each notification card **③** identifies the affected resource and its new state:

- **Saved in Design State** — the service configuration is drafted and stored, awaiting your final review and order submission.
- **Ready to Patch state** — your port or connection has been deployed in the physical network and is ready for cross-connect or patch cabling.
- **Subscription created successfully** — confirms an active billing subscription has been provisioned and linked to your service.

To learn what each lifecycle milestone means and how orders progress, see [Understanding Service Status](/service-management/service-status).

## VISTA Performance Alerts

Switching to the **Alerts** tab reveals active operational alerts triggered when monitored telemetry crosses your configured thresholds in VISTA:

- **Severity Badges ①** — flags each issue as **Critical** (red diamond) or **Info** (blue circle), along with the exact time raised and ongoing duration (e.g. _7m ongoing_).
- **Threshold Details ②** — provides a plain-language summary of what happened (e.g. availability dropping below your 95% or 100% alert level) and clearly states the **Affected Service** ID.
- **Direct Action Shortcuts ③** — provides one-click shortcuts right on the alert card: **View alert →** jumps to the rule definition under Settings > Manage Alerts, while **Check performance →** opens the live telemetry dashboards in VISTA for that specific service.

![Alerts tab displaying VISTA performance alerts and action shortcuts](/images/screenshots/notifications/02-alerts-tab.jpg)

<Tip>
  Clicking **Check performance →** directly opens the real-time VISTA graphs for that affected connection, saving you from having to search for the service manually. See [VISTA](/vista/overview) for configuring metrics and thresholds.
</Tip>

## Managing the Feed

Both the Alerts and Notifications tabs include identical management controls at the top **②**:

- **All vs. Unread** — toggle to **Unread** to isolate only items you haven't acknowledged yet, keeping your focus on immediate actions.
- **Mark All as Read** — clears the unread badge and counter without having to open each item individually.
- **Historical Feed** — read notifications stay grouped by day (e.g. _Today_, _Yesterday_) even after acknowledgement, allowing you to easily scroll back through recent changes.
- **Dismiss Drawer** — click the close icon (**✕**) in the top-right corner or click anywhere outside the drawer to return to your current screen.
